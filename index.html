Trip Planner

A city trip planner that solves the scheduling problem behind an itinerary. It scores 128 catalogued places against your interests, fits them to a budget and a number of days, clusters them into neighbourhoods, and routes each day with a nearest-neighbour and 2-opt search laid against real opening hours — then reports what each pass optimised, so the result can be checked rather than taken on trust.
